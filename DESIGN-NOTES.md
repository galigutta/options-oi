# Design notes — mobile-first pass

Generated by `open_interest/build_site.py`; edit that file, not the HTML here.

## What changed for phones

- **Type & targets:** 16px base font; table cells 14–15px with 10px padding. Back link and symbol cards stay at least 44px tall.
- **Latest day spotlight:** a card above the history table with large tiles for spot, 30-day IV, and the -50 / -20 / 0 / +20 / +50 shocks (only the ones present). Values come from the most complete row for the newest date, shown in short form (e.g. `−111.4M`) with the implied price (`@ 130.88`). Tiles are green or red by sign. On phones they sit two per row, and the at-spot tile spans the full width.
- **Sign colors in tables:** shock ladder cells and numeric cells in the expiry pivot get `num-pos` / `num-neg`.
- **Thinner sticky column:** the `Symbol` column has the `.col-sym` class and is hidden at ≤720px, since it always matches the page ticker. The sticky first column has a right border and a soft shadow so it stays separate while scrolling.
- **Swipe cues:** tables scroll inside `.tscroll` with a fade on the right edge (`.tbox::after`). The swipe hint is now accent-colored and also appears on the pivot. The card header no longer scrolls sideways with the table.
- **Safe areas:** `.wrap` padding uses `env(safe-area-inset-left/right/bottom)` for notched phones in landscape. Every page, including stub pages, now has `viewport-fit=cover`.
- **Landing page:** symbols are split into "With data" (highlighted cards) and "Awaiting snapshot" (dashed, dimmed cards). Watchlist order is kept within each group.
- **Honest counts:** the "trading day(s)" badge counts distinct dates, not CSV rows. SPCX has 3 rows for 2026-09-17, which is 1 day.

## Remaining gaps

- **One day of history.** SPCX only has data for 2026-09-17, so there is no trend to show yet. A sparkline or change-from-yesterday on the tiles would make sense after several days of snapshots. None has been invented.
- **Duplicate same-day rows.** `SPCX-index.csv` has 3 rows for one date, and 2 of them have empty shock values. The history table still shows all three. The real fix belongs in `oi.py` (append once per day, or upsert).
- **Most tickers are empty.** 10 of 11 watchlist symbols have no snapshot and only get stub pages.
- **Pivot tables are still wide.** The expiry × price pivot has 7+ price columns and many expiries. It scrolls fine, but you can't read it at a glance on a phone. Options: collapse to the nearest few expiries with a "show all" toggle, show a card per expiry, or render a heatmap.
- **Pivot header.** The pandas MultiIndex header (`netHedge` / `Price` / `Expiry` rows) wastes vertical space. Flattening it in `oi.py` or in the extractor would help.
- **No light mode or `prefers-reduced-motion` handling** (there are no animations yet, so the second doesn't matter today).
